Synopsis

In Ryali, a village of canals, coconut groves and long memories, Madhava Raju walks home from his ethanol plant one night and is ambushed by seven men on the field road. He walks away with a split eyebrow, bruised ribs and a calm that nobody can explain.

Dr. Sukanya, the young physician at nearby Ajay Hospital, is the one who bandages him. She notices that his composure does not match his injuries. The man behind the attack, Suribabu, wants something Madhava refuses to sell, and has always got what he wanted by threatening other people's families.

As Sankranti turns the village into a festival of rangoli, pongal and processions, two people who barely speak find they cannot stop noticing each other. Meanwhile an old, unsolved case at an abandoned bungalow begins to travel through Ryali as rumour. Some truths, as it turns out, travel furthest in silence.

Main Characters

Madhava Raju

A quiet, self-made businessman in his late twenties, who runs a dairy, a biogas plant and an ethanol plant. He says most of what he thinks with his eyes rather than his mouth, and he is never as surprised as he should be.

Dr. Sukanya

A sharp, warm-hearted young doctor from Ryali. She has a doctor's eye for what a patient is not saying, and no plan to fall for one.

Suribabu

A crooked local businessman who wants a share of someone else's success, on terms only he would call fair. He has done it before, and he is used to it working.

Devendra

The Sub-Inspector who has never let go of an old case. He asks polite questions and remembers the answers.

Genres & Themes

Genres: Rural Thriller, Romance, Crime, Family Drama

Themes: Silence and Secrets, Power and Its Abuse, Family Loyalty, Love in a Small Village, Justice Outside the Law
